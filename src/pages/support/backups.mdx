While your Bleep data is safely stored on your device and on your iCloud Drive, having a local copy can give you extra peace of mind and be helpful in other areas, like starting fresh on Bleep without permanently deleting your data.

## How do I export my data?

Head over to Settings, hit _Export…_, and choose a destination for your backup. It should take less than a minute to complete.

## What does Bleep export?

A folder containing:

* Your notes (as `.md`)
* Your bookmarks (as `.md`)
* Your images (in their original format)
* Your rooms (as folders)

## Some things to keep in mind

* Loose items (not inside of any Room) will be exported at the root of the exported folder.
* Items inside of one or more Rooms will be exported inside of each of the rooms they belong to.

## How do I restore from a backup?

Easy! Hit _Import…_ in Settings and choose a folder containing your export. Note: Bleep does not currently keep track of what's already been imported.
