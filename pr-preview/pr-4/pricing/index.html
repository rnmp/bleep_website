Questions about pricing

🎁 Can I use Bleep for free?
Yes. The free plan includes unlimited items and rooms, the browser extension, offline access, and sync across devices via iCloud.
👀 Can I try Pro before paying?
Yes! You'll enter a free 7-day trial before you're build on the yearly or monthly plan.
⚡️ What does Pro add?
Pro adds putting items in multiple rooms, pinned items, bulk editing, customized bookmarks, and nested rooms.
💳 How is Pro billed?
Pro is $3.99 billed monthly, $29.99 billed yearly, or $99.99 once for lifetime. Prices in USD and vary per region.
